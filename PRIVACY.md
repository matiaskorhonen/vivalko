# Vivalko privacy policy

Vivalko collects no personal data and has no server of its own. It has no analytics or tracking.

- **What it reads:** wine names, producers, categories and countries shown on alko.fi pages.
- **Where that goes:** this wine information is sent to Vivino's search (hosted on Algolia) and to vivino.com to look up ratings, grape names and bottle images. Vivino's privacy policy covers those requests.
- **What it stores:** lookup results are cached locally in your browser for up to 7 days (the grape list for up to a month). This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the extension.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/matiaskorhonen/vivalko/issues
